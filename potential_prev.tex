Moreover, in determining the skill, the variance of the skill distribution, $\mathrm{Var}[s] = \omega_{s}$, controls the shape of the underlying skill distribution from which a trader draws the actual skill. A trader equipped with a large $\omega_{s}$ draws her skill from a distribution with a wider scope, making her more likely to acquire a high skill level, $|s|$. Therefore, we refer to $\omega_{s}$ as the trader's \textit{skill potential}. In practical terms, $\omega_{s}$ can reflect factors such as the trader’s investment in higher education in finance and economics, which broadens the range of skills available to her. In Section \ref{sec:skillacquisition}, we endogenize $\omega_{s}$ by incorporating costly skill acquisition by traders.
