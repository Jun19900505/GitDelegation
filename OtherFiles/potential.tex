\paragraph{\normalfont{\it{Skill acquisition.}}} 
In determining the skill, $Var(\eta) = \sigma_{\eta}^2$  controls the shape of the underlying skill distribution from which a trader draws the actual skill. In what follows, we refer to $\sigma_{\eta}^2$ as the trader's \textit{skill potential}: a high-potential trader, being equipped with a large $\sigma_{\eta}^2$, is more likely to acquire a higher skill, $|\eta|$. Although the baseline analyses take this parameter as exogenously given, Section XXX considers an endogenous $\sigma_{\eta}^2$, describing a trader's costly skill acquisition to increase a chance of obtaining a high skill.
